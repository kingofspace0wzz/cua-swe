Help — artwork and gestures

Nested starts unselected: click Blue to select it. Drag a side handle to change only that dimension along Blue's current axes in its containing frame; corners change both dimensions independently. The opposite edge or corner stays fixed, as does your initial grab offset in the resized directions. The rotation handle turns Blue in that same frame around its current center, preserving its dimensions. Ancestor stretching can change apparent screen angles and lengths; this is local-frame rotation, not rigid scene rotation.

A side or corner may pass through its opposite edge or corner, collapse, and expand beyond it; corner axes cross independently. During drags and after ordinary release, Blue's paint, red outline and usable handles agree. Release commits without a jump. A newly grabbed visible handle resizes the resulting shape, and a later fill drag moves the whole shape rigidly. Amber, both frames, selection and camera values stay fixed.

All positions below are centers in the immediate parent's coordinates. Outer gray frame: scene center (200,150), size 180×120, angle 25°, scale (1.2,0.8). Inner gray frame: outer-local center (0,0), size 120×80, angle −20°, unit scale. Blue: inner-local center (0,0), size 72×44, angle 15°, center origin. Amber: outer-local center (60,0), size 16×16, angle 0°, unit scale. Frames and Amber are not editable.

Single keeps the original selected artwork and the same handle/fill gestures without frames. Use the Camera selector for the existing A, B, C and D presets and the Artwork selector for Nested or Single. Camera transforms can project a rectangle as a parallelogram; its outline follows the projected edges.
