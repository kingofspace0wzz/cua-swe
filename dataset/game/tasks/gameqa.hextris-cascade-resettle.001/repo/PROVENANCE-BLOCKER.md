# Distribution blocker: incomplete license and asset provenance

`RIGHTS.md` records the identified Hextris implementation as
`GPL-3.0-or-later`, but the discovery snapshot does not include:

1. the full GPL license text;
2. an exact revision of `https://github.com/Hextris/hextris`;
3. complete copyright and license records for the bundled fonts, images, and
   vendored JavaScript files; or
4. a reconciliation between the GameWorld-Games noncommercial disclaimer and
   the permissions and no-additional-restrictions terms of the GPL.

The source snapshot and this notice may be used for quarantined internal
construction and static review. Do not publish, promote, or redistribute this
candidate until the full license text and asset provenance have been audited
and added.
